Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025‑01‑01

Overview

Glass 2048 is designed with privacy in mind. We do not create user accounts or collect personally identifiable information on our servers.

Data we collect

  • In‑App Purchases — Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple’s StoreKit. We don’t receive your payment information.
  • Advertising (AdMob) — If ads are enabled and you consent, Google AdMob may process device information (such as IP address, advertising identifier) to deliver and measure ads. Consent is managed via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). If you buy “Remove Ads”, we stop making ad requests.
  • Diagnostics — We may receive aggregated crash logs and performance data provided by Apple to improve stability.

How we use data

  • Deliver core features (purchases, gameplay).
  • Show ads only when enabled and consented.
  • Improve quality via anonymized diagnostics.

Sharing

We don’t sell your data. Limited data may be shared with Google AdMob strictly for advertising when enabled. See Google’s Advertising Policy.

Your choices

  • Manage ad consent in the app’s Settings → Advertising.
  • Remove ads permanently via the “Remove Ads” in‑app purchase.

Children

Glass 2048 is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes
