FAQ

  • How do I play? Swipe in any direction. Matching numbers merge into a higher tile. Plan chains to climb to 2048 and beyond.
  • Sounds & Haptics? Settings → Sounds / Haptics. Sounds respect Silent Mode.
  • Reduce Motion / High‑Contrast? Settings → Accessibility → toggles inside the app.
  • Remove Ads? Settings → Advertising → Remove Ads. The purchase is non‑consumable and supports Family Sharing.
  • Restore purchase? On a new device, open Settings → Advertising → Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.
